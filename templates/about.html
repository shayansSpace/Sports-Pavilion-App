We are Sports Pavilion

Sports Pavilion is your home for soccer, cricket, football, and futsal. Our courts are built for both casual play and competitive matches, with flexible booking options that fit your schedule.

You can book a court in minutes for a one-off game, or become a member for priority slots, member pricing, and access to community events. Whether you are training a team, organizing a friendly match, or just looking for a great place to play, we make it simple to get on the court.

From quick bookings to full membership, Sports Pavilion is here to help you play more, connect with other players, and enjoy the game you love.
